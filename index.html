BEFORE YOU CALL

Straight answers to common questions.

Can I request more photos or a walk-around video?

Yes. Send the vehicle name or stock number and tell the team what you want to see more closely. A personal walk-around video is available on request.

What does the displayed price include?

The published asking price appears with every vehicle. Availability, taxes, fees, and any delivery cost are confirmed separately with B&B.

Can I discuss financing?

Use the form and select “Financing.” Available terms depend on the buyer and the transaction; no approval is promised by this page.

Can the vehicle be delivered?

Delivery planning is available. Share your destination ZIP code so the team can discuss timing and a route-specific quote.
